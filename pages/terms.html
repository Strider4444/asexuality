Terms you should know

Sex Negative vs Sex Repulsed

Sex Negative: The belief that sex is a generally negative topic in any context, and that it should not be discussed or partaken in, excluding for reproduction.

Sex Repulsed: Having negative feelings towards oneself being included in sexual acts. This can sometimes also be referred to as sex averse.

Sex Neutral vs Sex Indifferent

Sex Neutral: Believing that sex is neither a positive or a negative topic.

Sex Indifferent: Having a neutral stance about partaking in sexual activities. Doesn't mind having sex, but also doesn't care if no sex happens.

Sex Positive vs Sex Favourable

Sex Positive: The belief that sex as a topic should be discussed and that people should pursue sexual activities if they want to.

Sex Favourable: Having a desire to participate in sexual activities, regardless of having sexual attraction towards the people involved.
