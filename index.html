Asexuality

What is it, really?

Asexuality is an orientation which describes people experience little to no sexual attraction towards other people. It does not always mean a total lack of attraction, it can also apply to someone only feels limited attraction, or only experiences attraction in certain cases.

Asexuality is not lack of libido, and it is not the choice to avoid sexual relationships.

Asexuality is a spectrum, and everyone will have a different experience and identity within this spectrum!
